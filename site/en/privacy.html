Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how IDN Technology Group Inc. handles information when providing the IDN Code website, desktop app and related account services.

Effective date: October 5, 2026

Information We Collect

We may collect account email, login status, device identifiers, download waitlist email, service usage records, task content submitted by users and technical logs needed to provide the service.

How We Use Information

We use information to provide account login, release notices, service security, product features, troubleshooting, user support and records required for compliance.

Local Execution and Permissions

IDN Code desktop tasks run in the local computer environment authorized by the user. Project files, browser, app and system permissions are controlled by the user on the device; except for content the user submits, local code and files are not hosted remotely.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share limited information only when needed to provide the service, comply with law, protect users and service security, or with clear user authorization.

Model Channels and Third Parties

With the official IDN compute channel, requests are handled by IDN or its processors. When you configure your own compatible API Key, requests are handled by the provider you choose under their terms, so please review their privacy policy as well.

User Choices

Users may choose not to submit a download waitlist email and may manage login status, device connections and available service settings through account features.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, but no internet or local software system can be guaranteed to be absolutely secure.

Contact

To request access, updates or deletion of relevant information, contact us at support@idncode.com or through the official support channel of IDN Technology Group Inc.
